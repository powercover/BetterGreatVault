**See exactly what your Great Vault can give before you choose.**

Better Great Vault turns the Great Vault into a planning tool. Every slot shows how far along it is
and the **exact item level** it awards, and opens onto a reel of everything it can drop. A
searchable loot table and a season-wide loot database list every reward for any class and
specialization, so you know what to run next.

![Blizzard's Great Vault and the same vault with Better Great Vault](IMAGE_COMPARE)

## Highlights

- **Exact item levels, never guesses.** Taken from the game's own reward data, including the
  vault's rule for a raid's last two bosses. When the game can't confirm a level, the addon says so.
- **Loot reels.** Point at an unlocked slot and it opens onto a reel of the loot it can award,
  colored by Best-in-Slot tier.
- **An opening for every specialization.** 40 animations, one for each specialization, plus three
  extras.
- **Your rewards, revealed.** At the vault, every slot holding a reward opens and lands on it,
  with its name and item level. Choosing works exactly as in Blizzard's vault.
- **Loot table and loot database.** Every reward at its exact item level, with search, slot and
  stat filters, set bonuses and item comparison.
- **Light by design.** Nothing runs every frame unless something on screen is moving. No other
  addons needed.
- **11 languages**, including Ukrainian.

## On the Great Vault

- **Progress and reward on every slot:** `3 / 4 Raid Bosses`, then the reward's upgrade track and
  exact item level once it unlocks.
- **Detailed slot tooltips:** what counts toward the slot (bosses killed, Mythic+ runs, world
  activity tiers), what the next slot needs, and the next step up in reward.
- **Best-in-Slot tiers:** items rated S, A, B, C or D for your loot specialization, from Wowhead's
  class guides.
- **Loot spec button:** see and change the specialization your vault loot is filtered by, right on
  the vault.
- **Sad gates:** point at **Collect** and your completed slots look disappointed. The slot holding
  your best reward takes it hardest. You can turn this off.

## Loot table and loot database

Left-click an unlocked slot, or middle-click the minimap button, to list every reward it can give:
your class set first, then each raid boss or dungeon, every item at the exact item level the slot
awards. Columns for Best-in-Slot tier, item level, secondary stats, and slot with armor type; a
search box, a gear slot filter and a stat filter; tooltips compared with your gear; Shift-click to
link an item in chat at that item level whenever the game can build that link.

![The loot table](IMAGE_LOOT_TABLE)

The **loot database** lists everything the Great Vault can award this season, as if all content
were done, for any class and specialization: your class set, every raid boss by difficulty, every
Mythic+ dungeon by keystone level, and world gear (delves, prey and world bosses) by tier.

![The loot database](IMAGE_DATABASE)

## Minimap button and more

- **An animated vault door on the minimap.** Hover it for this week's vault at a glance: each
  slot's item level or progress and the time to the weekly reset. Also in the addon compartment.
- **A data bar feed** for ElvUI, Titan Panel, ChocolateBar and other data bars, when another addon
  has loaded LibDataBroker.
- **A reminder in chat** when rewards are waiting, and `/bgv status` for this week's vault in chat.
- **Your look:** text size from -5 to +5, an accent color (your specialization's or one you pick),
  key bindings, and settings saved per character.

![The minimap button's popup: this week's vault at a glance](IMAGE_MINIMAP)

## Commands

- `/bgv`: the version and the list of commands
- `/bgv status`: this week's Great Vault in chat
- `/bgv db`: open or close the loot database
- `/bgv perf`: what the addon costs (CPU, memory, load time)

Everything else is in **Options → AddOns → Better Great Vault**, or right-click the minimap button.

## Languages

English, Deutsch, Español (Spain and Latin America), Français, Italiano, Português, Русский,
Українська, 한국어, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. The addon speaks the game's language; Ukrainian, which the
game doesn't have, is picked in the settings.

## Compatibility

Retail World of Warcraft 12.1 (Midnight). Works with addons that gather minimap buttons into a bar
or menu, such as EllesmereUI.
