# Better Great Vault 1.1.0

- **Settings that show where they apply.** Rest the pointer on an option in the settings, and a
  small sketch of the addon shows exactly what it changes, lit in your accent color: the Great
  Vault, the loot table, the minimap and its popup, Blizzard's addon menu or the chat. The
  opening options play your opening animation right in the sketch. Pointing changes nothing; a
  click does, and the sketch follows it at once.
- **What's new.** Click the version line in the settings' About, or type `/bgv news`, to see
  what's new. It never opens by itself.
