# Better Great Vault 1.1.1

- **Fixed:** the loot database's Tier set source showed no pieces for classes other than your own.
  Another class's set now lists its five pieces.
- **Next step in the minimap popup.** Under this week's slots, the popup shows the fewest runs
  that unlock a slot or raise a reward, with the exact item level the vault gives, for example
  "+12 or higher x1: Dungeons, slot 1, rises to 272 item level". It's also in `/bgv status`, and
  you can turn it off in the settings, under the popup's options.
